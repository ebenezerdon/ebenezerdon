### Deployed Solutions

```diff
+ CuriousLM (Local AI)
  - Free, private AI that runs entirely on your device. Chat, write, and work with your files
    with no account and no internet connection.

+ Curious 2B (AI Model)
  - My own on-device language model: Qwen3.5 2B fine-tuned for the work CuriousLM does on a
    phone, reading your calendar and notifications and calling tools. Runs locally with
    llama.cpp, in eight languages. 93.6% on held-out phone and calendar turns against 85.4%
    for the base model, and 94.2% against 64.5% on notification reading.

+ Asụsụ Ọma (Igbo Learning App)
  - A free Igbo learning app with guided lessons and real Igbo voices, for anyone who wants
    the language, at home or abroad.

+ CopyMe (Teleprompter)
  - A teleprompter in your browser. It scrolls at your pace or follows your voice as you
    speak, and any other device is your remote.

+ Teda (AI App Builder)
  - Describe an app in plain words and Teda builds it in your browser.

+ Faye (Privacy-First Period Tracker)
  - A free period tracker that keeps everything on your device. No account, no server,
    no sharing.

+ NewDev (Learning Platform)
  - A learning platform and community for people starting out in software.

+ NextJob (AI Job Platform)
  - Tracks applications, drafts cover letters, and preps you for interviews.

+ NPM Studio (VSCode/JetBrains)
  - Manage your npm packages directly from the editor. Find, install, update, or remove
    without touching the command line.

+ License Pilot (VSCode Extension)
  - Adds the right open-source license to your project in two clicks.

+ Offline AI Chat (Open Source)
  - A language model chat that runs entirely in your browser with WebLLM. Nothing leaves
```
